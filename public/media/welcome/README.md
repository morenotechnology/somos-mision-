Prompt final:

Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: a polished 16:9 welcome-slide background for the Colombian Christian community website "Red de los 5.000 Amigos", within the existing Somos Misión Colombia navy-and-gold visual identity.
Primary request: create one striking, welcoming, beautifully crafted illustration, not a website mockup. Deep midnight navy (#0c1940) textured paper, warm matte gold (#f4cc53), layered tactile fibers and softly illuminated woven ribbons that converge like a community coming together. Subtle Colombian botanical silhouettes at the far lower corners, restrained warm light flowing across the edges. Rich material texture, optimistic and sophisticated, not a sci-fi interface.
Composition: landscape 16:9. Preserve a large very dark, quiet central and left-central area (middle 65% of canvas) for a live HTML Spanish welcome headline to be placed later. Detail and luminous threadwork hug the outer edges, do not cross that headline-safe area. Works when scaled to a mobile landscape panel. Generous breathing room, excellent contrast for white/gold type.
Text: none; all lettering will be real accessible HTML.
Avoid: maps of Colombia or any map, eagles, birds, logos, badges, floating cards, numbers, UI, buttons, people, watermarks, purple, excessive particles, lens flares. No reproduction of a brand mark. This is a provisional welcome illustration, not factual photography.
